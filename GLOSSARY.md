# Free My TV

A home screen for Google TV and Android TV (and the same screen as an app on LG and Samsung) that replaces the
stock one and shows what matters for the day: apps, inputs, picks and widgets.

## Home screen

**Shelf**:
The glass bar with the person's chosen apps, always on screen.
_Avoid_: Dock, app row

**Picks**:
The titles recommended for right now, as three cards under the Explore, Movies and Shows tabs.
_Avoid_: Recommendations, suggestions, hero

**Drawer**:
The part of the home screen that slides up with ↓ on the Shelf and goes away with ↑ or Back.
_Avoid_: Panel, bottom sheet

**Home layout**:
Which content sits on top and which in the Drawer: For You on top (Picks on top, Widgets in the Drawer) or Widgets on
top (Widgets on top, Picks in the Drawer).
_Avoid_: Theme, mode

**Widget**:
A read-only card in the widget row (Weather, Quotes, Calendar), switched on or off in Home Settings.
_Avoid_: Panel, tile

## Calendar

**Calendar**:
The Widget with the person's next Appointments, read from their own calendar. Shown as "Agenda" in Portuguese.
_Avoid_: Schedule, events widget

**Calendar link**:
The secret iCal address of one of the person's calendars, which they paste into the settings file; it is secret.
_Avoid_: API URL, token, key

**Appointment**:
One entry of the Calendar, with a start and an end, or all-day.
_Avoid_: Event, meeting
